# Founding Members Programme — Conjugately

Canonical business terms. Decision date: 2026-08-12. Supersedes any earlier draft
(previous 250-spot draft was never committed and is void).

## Terms

| Cohort | Offer |
|---|---|
| Accounts 1–100 ("Founding Members") | Year 1 free, then 50% off standard pricing in perpetuity |
| Accounts 101+ | Standard pricing |

## Standard pricing

- $4.99 / month
- $29.99 / year

## Notes

- Founding status attaches to the account, is non-transferable, and applies to the
  member's selected language track.
- No billing exists in the app yet. When subscriptions ship on iOS they must use
  Apple In-App Purchase (App Store Guideline 3.1.1); the founding discount will be
  implemented as introductory/promotional offers on the IAP products.
- Counting starts from public App Store launch, ordered by account creation time.
